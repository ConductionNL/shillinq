# Screens

- ShDebiteurenbeheer: send a payment request for a case or intake and see when it is paid https://identity.conduction.nl/screens/board?id=shillinq/ShDebiteurenbeheer
- ShVerkoopfactuur: put a pay now link for iDEAL or card on the invoice https://identity.conduction.nl/screens/board?id=shillinq/ShVerkoopfactuur
- portaliq/Betalen: the resident pays the fee for their case through the payment link, with iDEAL or card (row cmp-cas-pay) (portaliq adopts this) https://identity.conduction.nl/screens/board?id=portaliq/Betalen
- portaliq/BetalingResultaat: the result after paying, paid or not, with the way back to the case (row cmp-cas-pay) (portaliq adopts this) https://identity.conduction.nl/screens/board?id=portaliq/BetalingResultaat
