The fallback is strictly bounded — single method, ~20 LOC, no orchestration — to prevent ADR-031 anti-pattern creep. Output of the sampling aggregation is renderable through the `steekproef-werkpapier` docudesk template (Task 8). Out of scope for this T2 spec-only envelope. (HANDOFF verified — sibling on dev)
