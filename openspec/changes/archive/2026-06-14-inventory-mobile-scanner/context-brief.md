# Inventory Mobile Scanner (PWA, offline)

## Purpose

Mobile app/PWA for receive/transfer/pick/count with offline mode. Critical for warehouse UX.

## Priority & Demand

- **Priority:** P0-must
- **Demand evidence:** 19/22 competitors
- **Dependencies:** inventory-stock-tracking, inventory-barcode-sku
