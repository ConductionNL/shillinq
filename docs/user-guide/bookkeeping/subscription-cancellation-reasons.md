# Cancellation reasons

When a recurring invoice profile (a subscription) ends, Shillinq asks why. The reasons add up on the **Cancellation reasons** report, so you can see which reasons cost the most monthly revenue.

## End a subscription with a reason

1. Open the subscription under **Sales > Recurring Invoices**.
2. Choose **End**.
3. Pick a **Cancellation reason** and, if you like, add a note.
4. Confirm.

Staff must pick a reason. **Not given** is not accepted when you end a subscription yourself.

| Reason | Use it when |
|---|---|
| Too expensive | the customer says the price is the reason |
| Not using it | the customer no longer uses the service |
| Switched provider | the customer moved to another supplier |
| Missing feature | something the customer needs is missing |
| Service quality | the customer was unhappy with the service |
| Business closed | the customer stopped trading |
| Other | none of the above; say why in the note |
| Not given | only for a customer who ended it without a reason |

Shillinq records who ended the subscription (staff or the customer), when it was asked, and the day it takes effect: the day before the next invoice would have been made. A subscription that reaches its own end, such as its last occurrence, records no reason.

## Customers ending their own subscription

A customer with a portal account sees their active and paused subscriptions in the portal and can end one there. They may leave the reason out. Shillinq checks that the subscription bills that customer before ending it; another customer's subscription is refused.

For this, each subscription is linked to its customer. When you upgrade, Shillinq links existing subscriptions to the customer with the same contact reference or e-mail address. The upgrade log names any subscription it could not link; open it and choose the customer by hand. Invoices made from a linked subscription bill that customer.

## The Cancellation reasons report

Open **Reports** and choose the **Cancellation reasons** card. The page shows:

- **Ended subscriptions per quarter**: how many subscriptions ended in each quarter.
- **Lost monthly amount per reason**: the monthly amount the ended subscriptions billed, added up per reason.
- **Ended subscriptions**: the latest ended subscriptions with their quarter, reason and monthly amount. Choose a row to open it.
