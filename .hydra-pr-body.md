## Summary

Introduces the `FiscalYear` register with a declarative `open → closing → closed → reopened` lifecycle for Shillinq's Tier 4 year-end close capability. The `beginClose` transition requires all T3 fiscal periods to be closed, emits a retained-earnings transfer `JournalEntry` (manual sub-type), and fires a `bookkeeping.fiscalyear.closed` CloudEvent for dimensional rollover. The `completeClose` transition emits the next-year opening-balance `JournalEntry` (balance-sheet accounts only) and auto-creates the next `FiscalYear` record. The `reopen` transition is admin-only (`x-openregister-rbac`), requires a non-empty `reopenReason`, and emits two reversing `JournalEntry` records for audit-pair traceability. No PHP `YearEndCloseService` class is authored — all logic is schema-declarative per ADR-031.
